Privacy & SMS Policy

MadVylage / Dylan Martinez Consulting · Last updated August 2026

MadVylage builds and operates software for its clients. We collect only the information needed to deliver those services and we do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing.

What we collect

  • Client business data processed on behalf of our clients inside the applications we build, under their direction.
  • Contact details (name, email, phone) for the people who use those applications, provided by them or by our clients.

SMS / text messaging

Applications we operate send SMS notifications for operational alerts only, for example staff shift reports and time-sensitive facility alerts. Messages go exclusively to team members who have asked to receive them.

  • Consent is collected directly from each recipient before their number is added; numbers are never purchased, rented, or shared.
  • Alerts are sent from our toll-free number, (877) 359-8918.
  • Message frequency varies with operational activity.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out immediately, or HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Mobile numbers and opt-in status are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

How SMS consent is collected

Consent is obtained verbally, in person or by phone, before a number is added to any alert list, using the following script. Each consent is logged with the recipient's name, phone number, and the date it was given.

"As part of working with us, our system will send you operational text alerts - things like shift reports, health alerts, and system notices - at your cell number, from our toll-free number (877) 359-8918. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Our privacy and SMS policy is at madvylage.com/privacy.html. Are you OK receiving these messages?"

Email

Client-facing email (records, receipts, announcements) is sent on behalf of our clients to their customers, with unsubscribe honored on every non-transactional message.

Data retention & security

Data lives in access-controlled databases operated for each client, with routine backups. We retain it for as long as the client engagement requires and delete it on request.
